FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your logs are saved only in this browser on your device. Bloomly has no server, no accounts and no analytics, so your data is never sent anywhere. Anyone who can use your browser could open the app, so keep your device locked.

What goes on BOT Chain?

Only a generic daily check-in from your wallet address. The contract stores your streak count and the day number of your last check-in. No dates, symptoms, pregnancy details or hashes of them are ever sent.

Can I use it as birth control?

No. Bloomly gives estimates for information only. It is not medical advice and must not be used as birth control. Talk to a doctor or midwife about contraception.

How are predictions made?

Bloomly averages up to your last 6 logged cycles (28 days until you've logged at least 2). Your next period is your last start plus that length. Ovulation is estimated 14 days before, and the fertile window runs from 5 days before to 1 day after. If your cycles vary by more than 7 days, we'll tell you predictions are less certain.

What if I clear my browser or change phones?

Your logs live only in this browser, so clearing its data removes them. Export a backup from the Me tab now and then, and import it on any device to carry on where you left off.

Do I need a wallet?

Only for the self-care streak. All four trackers work without a wallet. If you'd like a streak, connect MetaMask and Bloomly will switch it to BOT Chain for you.
